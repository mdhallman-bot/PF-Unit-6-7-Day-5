Phase 2 · 15–30 min

2. Sell Your Skills

A résumé provides evidence of qualifications. A cover letter explains the connection to a particular role.

Compare: “I am a hard worker” versus “Coordinated supplies for a 20-person school event and met every deadline.” The second answer supplies evidence.

MagicSchool AI Résumé Coach — Ask → Refine → Verify → Decide

First use your own experience above. Then open our MagicSchool student activity ↗. Create your own prompt asking for a résumé bullet tailored to the job posting. Include what you actually did, the skill it demonstrates and this instruction: Do not invent duties, numbers, results or qualifications. Do not enter names or personal contact details.

Own Your Words checkpoint

Imagine an interviewer asks: “Tell me more about this résumé statement.” Explain what you actually did in 1–2 sentences. You should be able to support every claim in your final bullet.

Quick self-check: Is it accurate? Relevant to the posting? Defensible with a true example? If not, revise your bullet before continuing.
